D+Q 가 노쇠 백신 마우스의 CD8 T cell 면역우세를 재구성한다 D+Q reshapes CD8 T cell immunodominance in aged vaccinated mice

Cadar AN & Bartley JM · Aging Cell 2025;25(1):e70345 · 🧪 research
DOI: 10.1111/acel.70345 · PMC: PMC12748518 · Published 2025-12-29
한 줄:One-liner: D+Q (5/50 mg/kg, 사용자 protocol 과 동일 dose) 가 노쇠 마우스 백신 후 CD8 T cell repertoire 를 NP→PA 쪽으로 *재배치* 하지만 *viral clearance 나 lung pathology 는 개선 안 함* — senolysis 가 아닌 immunomodulation 으로 작동한다는 직접 증거. D+Q (5/50 mg/kg, same dose as user's protocol) reorganizes post-vaccination CD8 T cell immunodominance from NP toward PA epitopes in aged mice — but does NOT improve viral clearance or lung pathology, providing direct evidence that D+Q acts via immunomodulation rather than canonical senolysis.

Results Deep Dive — 3 핵심 findingResults Deep Dive — 3 core findings

1. D+Q 가 백신 + 감염의 primary outcome 을 개선 못 함D+Q fails to improve the primary vaccine + infection outcomes

Veh-vaccinated 와 D+Q-vaccinated 노쇠 마우스가 viral load (lung qPCR), lung histopathology, weight loss 모두 차이 없음. "minimal impact on overall vaccination and flu outcomes" (논문 abstract). senolytic 의 *전통적 expectation* (senescent cell 제거 → 면역 회춘 → clearance 향상) 이 이 모델에서는 fail.Vehicle-vaccinated and D+Q-vaccinated aged mice showed no differences in viral load (lung qPCR), lung histopathology, or weight loss. "Minimal impact on overall vaccination and flu outcomes" (paper abstract). The conventional senolytic expectation (clear senescent cells → rejuvenate immunity → improve clearance) fails in this model.

2. ★ Immunodominance hierarchy 가 NP→PA 로 reorganize★ Immunodominance hierarchy reorganizes from NP to PA

백신은 NP-protein 으로 보냈는데도 D+Q 처리군의 lung CD8 T cell 응답이 NP-specific 감소 + PA-specific 증가 (12 DPI). 즉 D+Q 가 *immune repertoire 의 epitope 우세 순위* 를 바꿈. 이게 efficacy 로 이어지진 않지만 — *senolytic 이 immune system 의 어느 axis 에 작용하는지* 의 mechanism-level 흔적.Despite NP-protein vaccination, D+Q-treated lung CD8 responses showed decreased NP-specific + increased PA-specific (12 DPI). D+Q reorganizes the *epitope dominance hierarchy* of the immune repertoire. This doesn't translate to better outcome, but it's a mechanism-level fingerprint of which immune axis senolytic touches.

3. Serum total IgG 증가 — late-phase humoral effectIncreased serum total IgG — late-phase humoral effect

20 DPI 에 D+Q-vaccinated aged 마우스의 serum anti-PR8 IgG 가 증가 (6/12/15 DPI 에는 차이 없음). 즉 D+Q 효과가 *delayed humoral immunity* 차원에서 나타남 — initial response 가 아니라 후기 antibody class switching/affinity maturation 가능성.At 20 DPI, serum anti-PR8 IgG was elevated in D+Q-vaccinated aged mice (no difference at 6/12/15 DPI). D+Q effect manifests in *delayed humoral immunity* — possibly affecting late-phase class switching or affinity maturation rather than initial response.

+ 반대 흐름 — Unvaccinated D+Q 는 *clearance 악화*+ Counter-flow — Unvaccinated D+Q shows *worsened clearance*

백신 없이 D+Q 만 받은 노쇠 마우스는 15 DPI 에 viral clearance *감소* + PA-specific CD8 *감소*. 즉 senolytic 이 *immune cell 의 정상 function* 도 일부 손상시킬 수 있음을 시사 — context dependence 가 강함.Unvaccinated D+Q-treated aged mice showed *impaired* viral clearance at 15 DPI plus *decreased* PA-specific CD8 responses. Suggests senolytic can also impair normal immune cell function — context dependence is strong.
