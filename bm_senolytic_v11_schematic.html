Federation architecture

Aged murine bone marrow contains a 4-node bidirectional federation comprising MSC, myeloid effectors (predominantly neutrophils), and HSPC. Senolytic D+Q reshapes this federation through four directional axes simultaneously, with the neutrophil emerging as the federation's mediator hub (Step 0 dominant source and Step 3 dominant target). Step 3 (MSC → myeloid) constitutes the v11 novel finding that closes the federation and demonstrates bidirectional rather than linear relay.

MSC sub-state coordinated rejuvenation

The MSC compartment operates as a combinatorial program switcher, not a unified bulk shift. The Osteo sub-state shows the de novo emergence of Bmp2 (Veh 0 → DQ 2.61 log₂CPM, FDR=0.011), Cxcl12, Lgals9, and seven other HSC-supportive ligands (binomial P=0.004). Adjacent sub-states show specialized changes: Perivascular Serpine1 collapse, CAR Dkk2 loss, providing sub-state-distributed program-switching evidence.

HSPC molecular consequences

Within the HSPC compartment, D+Q drives GMP expansion (11.4 → 19.7%, P=0.041) and CLP lymphoid priming (V(D)J commitment panel 7/7 expected direction, binomial P=0.008). Self-renewal transcription factors (Hlf, Mecom, Mpl, Hoxb5, Meis1) and AP-1 family members (Fos, Egr1) are significantly downregulated at the RNA level. Pseudotime analysis demonstrates LT-HSC root cohesion (KS P=9.7×10⁻⁶), a developmental rewind that is HSPC-specific.

Chromatin grammar preservation

Genome-wide RNA-ATAC concordance reveals weak coupling (Spearman r=0.207) with self-renewal TFs clustering in the RNA-down + ATAC-preserved quadrant. Direct motif-binding-site analysis (21 TFs, >100,000 motif peaks) confirms 88–93% of peaks show |Δ log₂FC| < 0.1 regardless of RNA direction. This bivalent priming signature provides the molecular basis for transient transcriptional reset without permanent erasure of stem identity.

One-line conclusion: Senolytic D+Q acts not through senescent cell clearance but through coordinated reset of a 4-node bidirectional federation, with MSC-Osteo as the transcriptional substrate, neutrophil as the federation mediator, HSPC as the downstream receiver, and chromatin grammar preserved as the "armed but quiet" molecular signature of reversible stem identity.
