Senolytic D+Q reshapes a 4-node bidirectional federation in aged murine bone marrow.

Single-cell RNA + scATAC integrated analysis (n=4 vs 4, 175,316 cells). 7-figure paper architecture, v11.

Federation architecture (4 axes)

Aged murine bone marrow contains a 4-node bidirectional federation comprising MSC, myeloid effectors (predominantly neutrophils), and HSPC. Senolytic D+Q reshapes this federation through four directional axes simultaneously: Step 1 (myeloid → MSC) shows SASP input collapse; Step 2 (MSC → HSPC) shows niche secretome swap with the single largest ΔP_int rise on Cxcl12-Cxcr4; Step 0 (myeloid → HSPC direct bypass) shows VISFATIN/IL1β collapse with Lgals9 as the unique riser; Step 3 (★ v11 novel) closes the federation via MSC → myeloid signaling with ADIPONECTIN and CXCL up and COMPLEMENT/GRN completely lost. The neutrophil emerges as the federation mediator hub, dominating Step 0 source and Step 3 target.

MSC sub-state coordinated rejuvenation

The MSC compartment operates as a combinatorial program switcher, not a unified bulk shift. The Osteo sub-state shows the dramatic transcriptional emergence of Bmp2 (Veh 0 → DQ 2.61 log₂CPM, FDR=0.011), Cxcl12, Lgals9, and seven other HSC-supportive ligands (binomial P=0.004). Adjacent sub-states show specialized changes: Perivascular Serpine1 collapse, CAR Dkk2 loss, providing axis-specific evidence for sub-state-distributed program switching.

HSPC molecular consequences

Within the HSPC compartment, D+Q drives GMP expansion (11.4 → 19.7%, P=0.041) and CLP lymphoid priming (V(D)J commitment panel 7/7 expected direction, binomial P=0.008). Self-renewal transcription factors (Hlf, Mecom, Mpl, Hoxb5, Meis1) and AP-1 family members (Fos −0.53 FDR=0.031, Egr1 −0.56 FDR=0.021) are significantly downregulated at the RNA level. Pseudotime analysis demonstrates LT-HSC root cohesion (KS P=9.72×10⁻⁶), a developmental rewind that is HSPC-specific and not observed at comparable magnitude in other lineage-progressive compartments.

Chromatin grammar preservation ("armed but quiet")

Genome-wide RNA-ATAC concordance analysis (13,971 genes) reveals weak coupling (Spearman r=0.207) with self-renewal TFs clustering in the RNA-down + ATAC-preserved quadrant. Direct motif-binding-site analysis confirms this dissociation: for 21 candidate TFs (over 100,000 motif-containing peaks), 88–93% of peaks show |Δ log₂FC| < 0.1 regardless of RNA direction. Self-renewal TFs (Hlf, Mecom, Hoxb5, Meis1, Hoxa9, Nfix, Erg) all show RNA strongly down (−0.09 to −0.55) but motif peak accessibility near zero (median Δ −0.013 to −0.024). This bivalent priming signature provides the molecular basis for transient transcriptional reset without permanent erasure of stem identity.

One-line conclusion: Senolytic D+Q acts not through senescent cell clearance but through coordinated reset of a 4-node bidirectional federation, with MSC-Osteo as the transcriptional substrate, neutrophil as the federation mediator, HSPC as the downstream receiver, and chromatin grammar preserved as the "armed but quiet" molecular signature of reversible stem identity.
